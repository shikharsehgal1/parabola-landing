Power, Accessible for Everyone.

In the AI and compute build-out, grids everywhere are under strain, and neither frontier labs nor households can manage what they consume. Grid Strategies puts U.S. demand up 16% by 2029; DOE and LBNL see data-centre use doubling or tripling by 2028.

Trading venues are closed to retail, and even professionals can't freely trade or directly hedge tail risk on today's primitive market structure.

Parabola turns that strain into something you can act on: an open exchange where anyone exposed to power, from a training campus to a household, can cap the hours that break their budget and trade the swings they can't consume around.

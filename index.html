04

One request. One price.

Send Parabola the hub, the window, the hours, the quantity, the strike and the cap. We mark the request against the curve, put it to the writers, and come back with a single premium the same business day.

01

Send the card

Or one sentence: "Cover 5 MWh an hour above $150, evenings, July through August, cap $400k."

02

Parabola marks and routes it

Expected payout under the curve, plus spread, goes to the writer panel as the reference. Writers quote their own premium.

03

Sign, post, settle

Standard term agreement. Buyer posts the premium, writer posts the cap less premium, both into escrow. Settles on the grid operator's published print. Parabola structures and matches the trade; it does not hold the risk.

Power price protection for frontier labs and retail

Cover the hours that spike your power bill.

Parabola publishes a daily probability curve for the price of power. Every number is published before the outcome is known and scored against the settled price the next morning.

A frontier training campus is the most price-inelastic load ever put on a grid. It cannot shift off-peak, cannot pause for scarcity pricing, and holds full draw through every hour that clears highest.

The top 200 hours are 2% of the year and 10% of the cost. They are the entire reason your annual power number cannot be forecast.

The tail doesn't make the bill big. It makes it unpredictable, and a budget you cannot commit to is a harder problem than a budget that is high.

We price those hours daily, and quote protection that narrows the range you have to plan against.

01

The number you can't commit to

Running flat, a 100 MW campus draws about 876,000 MWh a year: roughly $44M of energy at $50/MWh, plus about $12M of capacity at the $329.17/MW-day cap. Both lines move, and capacity is the one escalating fastest.

Depending on how the summer lands, that annual number swings by roughly $30M on identical load, with no decision you made. That spread is the problem, and it is priceable.
